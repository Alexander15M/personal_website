## Overview

SARA is a phone assistant for restaurants that take pickup orders. The caller talks through the order, SARA matches it to the Square menu, and a text message carries a Square payment link. The owner sets the greeting, the voice, and a reservation link in the app, so the counter does not have to take that call, total the ticket, or send the checkout.

## The problem

A pickup order still means a person on the phone while the counter is serving. They have to hear the items, match variations and modifiers, repeat a total, confirm a mobile number, and get the guest to pay. A reservation is a separate interruption, and a caller who switches to Spanish needs the same conversation in that language.

## What it does

### Set up the restaurant

- Sign up, then onboarding step 1 saves the account name and avatar.
- Step 2 creates the restaurant from its name. Step 3, Change Business Details, saves open and close times, days open, address, and a contact number.
- Settings, under Restaurant and then General, keeps the restaurant name, avatar, Square location, and those same business details.
- Settings, under Restaurant and then Billing, is where the subscription is turned on. If the restaurant has no phone number yet, Dashboard shows Create Assistant only while the subscription is active or trialing. Otherwise it links to Billing.

### Sync Square

- Integrations, titled Sync with POS systems, connects the Square account.
- After it connects, Add Menu with items from Square, or Update Menu with items from Square, copies catalog names, variations, prices, quantities, and item ids onto the assistant.
- Orders opens a sheet with order id, line items, total, and status, plus Refresh Orders and paging.
- Stock opens an inventory sheet with Refresh and paging.

### Open the phone line

- On Dashboard, Create Assistant asks for a US area code, buys a Twilio number, and attaches it to the assistant.
- The same screen shows Total Orders Placed, Total Calls, and Total Minutes from Square orders and stored calls.
- The call table lists start time and duration. View Call in Vapi opens that call in the Vapi dashboard.

### Configure the voice

- Playground, titled Configure your Tone & Voice, lists Cartesia and 11Labs voices. Play Sample plays the selected clip, and Save Voice Settings writes the choice.
- The page shows the assistant's phone number.
- First message, Restaurant Information, and Reservation Link are saved with Update Restaurant Information. The reservation field is for a link such as OpenTable or Resy.
- Those fields stay disabled until a menu is on the restaurant.

### Answer the call

- The marketing Demo page shows 570-569-1442, an orb that starts a browser call, and a sample menu with an English and Spanish toggle.
- A new assistant starts with a pickup greeting and can switch when the caller speaks Spanish. Playground can replace that first message.
- The ordering guide confirms each item, asks for a phone number, and sends payment only after the caller agrees.
- The order endpoint maps the items to Square catalog ids, creates a payment link, and texts it in English or Spanish. A saved reservation link can be texted the same way.

## Technical approach

The product is a pnpm workspace. The Next.js app is in `apps/web`, tRPC procedures are in `packages/api`, and Prisma models for users, restaurants, assistants, phone numbers, calls, and orders are in `packages/database` on PostgreSQL. Sessions use Lucia.

Create Assistant posts a Vapi assistant that runs on OpenAI gpt-4o, with a Deepgram multilingual transcriber and a Cartesia voice. Playground can switch that voice to another Cartesia voice or an 11Labs voice. The area-code search buys a US Twilio number and registers it on the Vapi assistant.

Square OAuth stores the merchant token on the user. Catalog items, orders, and inventory are read from Square, and the chosen Square location is stored on the restaurant. When Vapi calls `/api/vapi/create-order`, a second gpt-4o request maps the order onto catalog ids, Square creates the payment link, and Twilio sends the text. `/api/vapi/send-reservation-link` texts the restaurant's reservation URL. `/api/vapi/call-ended` stores the call's start, end, and Vapi call id.
