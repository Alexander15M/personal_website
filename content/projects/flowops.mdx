## Overview

FlowOps is the back office for a real estate agent. Deals, listings, showings, contacts, marketing projects and the calendar live in one app, and repetitive follow-up runs on automations.

## The Problem

An agent's week is spread across a CRM, a calendar, email, text threads and spreadsheets. Every new client means retyping details from a signed agreement. Every showing needs a confirmation text. Every closed deal needs a follow-up that's easy to forget.

## What It Does

### Client onboarding from a document
Upload a listing or buyer agreement and FlowOps pulls out the client details with AI and creates the profile.

### Deals and listings
Every property is tracked with its status (upcoming, active, inactive) and its client type (buyer or seller). Deals can be filtered, saved as templates, imported from CSV and exported.

### Projects and tasks
Marketing campaigns and transactions become projects, with tasks, assignees, due dates, documents and a milestone timeline. Projects can also generate marketing content with AI.

### Calendar
Showings, tasks and open houses share one calendar with separate toggles for each.

### Automations
Trigger-based workflows handle the routine work: a text to the requesting agent when a showing is confirmed, a welcome email and pre-marketing checklist when a listing is created, and a check-in task 30 days after a deal closes.
