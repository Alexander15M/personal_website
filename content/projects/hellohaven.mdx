## Overview

Haven is a personal AI for someone running their own day. The web app and the iPhone and Android apps share one account with text, email, and phone: +1 (435) 260-6500 and chat@hellohaven.ai. Lists, documents, reminders, and the profile on You stay on that account, so a request started in Chat can be opened later in Library. After a calendar or inbox is connected, Haven can read it and act on it once the person approves the action in Chat.

## The problem

A chat thread, a grocery list, a reminder, and an inbox usually live in different apps and do not share what was already said. None of those apps answer a text or a phone call, and starting a new thread drops the list, the document, and the profile. Haven keeps those pieces on one account that can be reached from the app, from email, or from the phone.

## What it does

### Chat, text, email, and phone

- Chat switches between Quick Ask, one running thread, and Conversations, separate threads. The box takes typed text, Dictate, and the + menu: Take Photo, Upload, From Haven's Brain, From Haven Docs, and From Haven Lists.
- The Chat reach menu opens Call Me, Text Me, Email Me, Voice Chat, and Contact Card. Call and text use +1 (435) 260-6500. Email opens chat@hellohaven.ai.
- Voice Chat starts a spoken session in the app. Feed suggestions either send a prompt into a new Chat conversation or open another screen, and each one can be dismissed.
- Library → Conversations lists threads and filters them by App, SMS, Group Chat, Voice, Email, Scheduled, or Shared.

### Lists, documents, and reminders

- Apps → Keep Organized opens Lists, Docs, Sticky Notes, Note Taker, and Agenda. Agenda is marked beta.
- Library shows the same Lists, Docs, and Sticky Notes, plus Images, Videos, and Audio.
- Library → Reminders → New reminder sets a repeat of Once, Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Yearly, and Send via Email, SMS, or Call.
- Before Haven sends a message or places a call, Chat shows an approval card. You → Your Haven → Advanced has the switch Let Haven act without asking.

### Apps and focus rooms

- Apps groups tiles into Keep Organized, Know Yourself, Focus & Wellbeing, and five skill rows: Run My Day, Shop, Book & Return, Make Something, Life & Home, and Play & Connect.
- Tapping a skill puts its prompt in the chat box so it can be edited before it is sent. A pin on a tile keeps it at the top of Apps on that device.
- Focus & Wellbeing opens Journal, Brain Dump, and Decide. Ground, Body Double, Worry Window, and Spark are marked beta.
- Know Yourself opens Patterns and YouIQ. Both tiles are marked beta.

### You, memory, and connected tools

- You has four tabs: About You, Your Haven, Account, and Help. Help & Community is also at /v3/feedback.
- Your Haven opens Haven's identity, Personality, How Haven reaches you, Display, Advanced, and Connected tools.
- Connected tools lists services to connect. Triggered automations can run from a connected tool, a Slack DM, or a schedule, and scheduled automations live under Library.
- Library also has Memory and Time Capsule, next to the conversations, lists, docs, and reminders.

## Technical approach

This repository is the Haven client and its Supabase backend. The Daylight interface is a React and TypeScript app built with Vite, with Capacitor projects for iPhone and Android. Supabase stores the account and the records behind conversations, lists, documents, reminders, and connected tools. Edge functions send reminder email through Resend and SMS or calls through Twilio. Chat turns, job streams, browser control, and user-added MCP servers go to a separate Haven runtime. In-app voice uses Vapi, and the Connected tools catalog is loaded through Composio.
